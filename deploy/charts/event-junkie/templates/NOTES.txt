EXPECTED ON A FIRST INSTALL: the BFF restarts for up to a minute or two.

The importer owns the Flyway migrations (ADR-005), so on an empty database the BFF starts before
the schema exists and its readiness probe fails until the importer has finished migrating. There is
no init container and no Helm hook ordering the two on purpose — Kubernetes already retries, and the
alternative is a startup dependency that has to be maintained forever. It self-heals. Treat it as an
incident only if it is still restarting after the importer's pod reports ready.
