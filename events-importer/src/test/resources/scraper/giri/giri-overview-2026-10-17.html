GIRI x CURRENTS

experimental, IDM, noise, video

Time
From 18:00 – end
Entry
Donation-based
Booking
RA RSVP required

CURRENTS opens a limited series of three live electronic music nights for artists working with their sonic heritage, curated by Memeshift of sōydivision and sibling experimental label L-KW.

The title refers both to the electrical energy flowing through musical machines and to the currents of time and ocean that move people and their entwined lineages across land and water.

For the first volume, BAERK., Leng Seong and Memeshift bring together maternally blessed cassette warmth, DV cam video and synthesised computer memory.

Kontralamakina presents KNTRLMKN09: Post mortem Reality, the debut label release by Greek DJ and producer Nases Morur.

Ten raw techno tracks move through deep atmospheres, driving beats, hypnotic ambiences, high-pitched drones, solid kicks and tense build-ups. Released as a limited-edition cassette and digital album, it captures his dark, modern approach to electronic music.

Nases Morur is joined by Thravsma and Fuerza Kontraria, founder and curator of Kontralamakina, for an evening of electronic, experimental and techno music.

Giri x Rawa Club: Mexican Ambient Night Vol. IV

Experimental / Downtempo

Time
From 18:00 – end
Entry
Donation-based
Booking
RA RSVP required

Berlin!

Join us one more time for the IV edition of our signature “Mexican Ambient Night” hosted by GIRI in the heart of Neukölln.

If you are familiar with this series of events you know that it’s all about inviting a line up of Mexican artists and giving them the space to explore a more introspective approach to their music practice. For this fourth edition we are glad to welcome:

hotel99* hailing all the way from the south corners of Mexico City and based in Barcelona, the musical alias of Diego Cuellar is built on experimentation, raw sampling and internet humor. Director at dublab.bcn and año bisiesto netlabel and radio show founder he moves between rhythms like drum and bass, footwork and boom bap.

Merma Suelo is a polyfaceted poet, journalist, performer and sound artist. They move between layers and dimensions to attend and transform living relational dynamics within ourselves and our environments, as well as their connection points and tools, resulting in what we call reality.

Parallel Nico works in the space between weather and memory drifting through ambient, techno, and breaks moving into each other, but never settling into one. Built entirely from sampled material, as a way to keep folding the past into the present, not nostalgia but a history still in motion.

And of course our co-founder and resident DJ Hidrataccioni, who will also be celebrating their birthday that day, will slow things down with the ongoing investigation on screwed boleros, jazz and other reminiscing Mexican songs.

Expect some good ambiente mexicano to the sounds of ambient, spoken word, downtempo, screwed boleros, cumbia rebajada, jazz, dub, hip hop and more.

Come thru and have fun with us on this special autumn celebration <3

Satelliet Studio presents a new visual research project connecting underground electronic music cultures with the graffiti world and urban traces. The exhibition brings together printed matter and apparel, creating a space for exchange and discovery, exploring the connections between images, objects and their surroundings. The gathering continues into an evening of music, featuring DJ sets and live performances.

GIRI X OCCULTISTS

post punk, techno, electro, raw trance

Time
From 18:00 – end
Entry
Donation-based
Booking

Occultists takes over Giri for an intimate showcase celebrating James Demon’s new EP, Spiritual Calling Card. Goth sounds meet techno with a dark edge, reflecting the label’s love of unconventional music. The evening features the worldwide premiere of the “Mother” music video, a collaboration with Nicky Miller, followed by a live performance of the track during James Demon’s DJ set. A guest DJ completes the lineup for a night of music, visuals and occult energy.
