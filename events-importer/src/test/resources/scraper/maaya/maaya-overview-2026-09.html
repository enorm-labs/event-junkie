MAAYA Berlin – Where Communities Meet

I AM BECAUSE WE ARE

Welcome to MAAYA, a multicultural event space in Berlin where communities, cultures and creative worlds come together.

Through music, art, food and shared experiences, MAAYA brings people together to connect, discover and celebrate. Our programme moves across cultures, communities and sounds, creating a space that reflects the diversity of Berlin.

More than just a venue, MAAYA is a meeting point for experiences and ideas. From community gatherings and cultural events to nightlife, open-air experiences and special occasions, there is always something new to discover.

EVENT SPACES

FOUR UNIQUE SPACES. ONE MAAYA

MAAYA is home to four distinct event spaces in Berlin, each with its own atmosphere and experience. From our Pool and open-air Garden to the Gallery and Backyard, every space offers a different setting for people to come together through music, culture, community and shared experiences.

From cultural gatherings and music events to corporate events, private celebrations and brand activations, MAAYA offers flexible spaces for many different occasions.

MAAYA Garden

MAAYA Garden is an open-air event space in Berlin for community gatherings, food, music and cultural events. A relaxed outdoor setting for meeting, connecting and experiencing the diversity of MAAYA.

MAAYA Pool

MAAYA Pool is a distinctive poolside event space in Berlin for open-air music, summer events, swimming, family experiences and special occasions. A unique setting at the heart of MAAYA.

MAAYA Gallery

MAAYA Gallery is our indoor event space for concerts, club nights, exhibitions, workshops, talks and cultural programming. It also offers a flexible setting for private events and special experiences.

MAAYA Backyard

MAAYA Backyard is a flexible outdoor event space for markets, workshops, cultural events, community gatherings, performances and brand activations, with room for many different formats and ideas.

FIND US IN BERLIN

MAAYA IN
FRIEDRICHSHAIN

Located at RAW-Gelände in Berlin-Friedrichshain, MAAYA brings multicultural events, community, music, art, food and experiences together in one of Berlin's most dynamic neighbourhoods.

With four indoor and outdoor spaces and easy access from Warschauer Straße, MAAYA is a meeting point for Berliners, international communities, event guests and visitors to the city.

THE VENUE AT A GLANCE

ONE VENUE.
MANY POSSIBILITIES.

In the heart of Berlin-Friedrichshain, MAAYA brings together more than 5,000 sqm of indoor and outdoor event space across four distinctive areas.

The Pool, Garden, Gallery and Backyard can be used individually or combined to create flexible settings for corporate events, brand activations, cultural programmes, private celebrations, workshops, productions and large-scale experiences.
