I AM BECAUSE WE ARE

Welcome to MAAYA – Berlin’s vibrant new cultural space inspired by African and Afro-Diasporic creativity.
MAAYA is a place where everyone is invited to explore, connect, and celebrate through art, music, food, and community.

Curated by a passionate and diverse team, MAAYA brings people together in an open and inspiring atmosphere whether you’re an artist, a culture lover, or just curious to experience something new.

More than just a venue, MAAYA is a living, breathing experience a hub where cultures meet, stories are shared, and unforgettable memories are made. Come as you are, and be part of the journey.

EVENT SPACES

FOUR UNIQUE SPACES. ONE MAAYA

MAAYA is home to four distinct event spaces, each offering its own atmosphere and experience. From tropical poolside settings and open-air gardens to creative indoor environments and versatile event areas, every space has been designed to bring people together through culture, community, and lasting memories.

Whether you're planning a corporate event, private celebration, cultural gathering, or summer festival, MAAYA offers the perfect setting for every occasion.

MAAYA Garden

Unwind in the MAAYA Garden, a modern twist on the classic biergarten perfect for relaxing, socializing, or co-working. Experience culture and community at its best!

MAAYA Pool

Around the MAAYA Pool, open-air music events, movie nights, and sports and family events take place. On hot days, the pool serves as a swimming oasis for young and old.

MAAYA Gallery

During the day, workshops, panel talks, theater performances, and art exhibitions are showcased in the indoor area. In the evenings, there are concerts and DJ events with artists from all over the world.

MAAYA Backyard

Explore the MAAYA Market, a publicly accessible area for diverse African and diaspora cuisine, unique art and fashion, live performances, and a kids’ play area.

AREAS

Surrounded by the historic walls of the former RAW site, the MAAYA now covers an area of over 5.000 square meters.

The heart of MAAYA is the 240sqm heated swimming pool, framed by a spacious sun deck made of white stone tiles and natural wooden planks. Sun beds, loungers and Mediterranean plants create a beach club atmosphere that you would otherwise only find on the Mediterranean.

The adjoining modern event hall is a former Reichsbahn maintenance depot that has been restored as an event location, bar and club. This is where industrial charm meets Berlin nightlife culture.
