8.10. THURSDAY psychedelic rock

Boo Centraal

Something old, something new, something borrowed - always Boo
Boo Centraal’s knack for marrying catchy melodies with complex harmony; within songs that can veer from tender ballads to sprawling post-punk infused soundscapes with (forked) tongue-in-cheek lyricism, make this band a captivating listen for any audience.
Boo Centraal is a four-piece art-rock band, based in Berlin. The band is led by vocalist/drummer Faolán Collins, with electric guitarist Fausto Cassarà, rhodes and synth player Alper Elmacı, and electric bassist Matilde Squillante supporting. Cassarà’s and Elmacı’s deep knowledge of sound, effects, and improvisational techniques create an otherworldly atmosphere over Collins’s compositions; while the rhythm section of Collins and Squillante provide a grooving, driving foundation for the band.

www.instagram.com/boocentraal

Noops   Indie Pop, Pop RnB

Born out of a real-life love story, Noops is a Berlin-based Pop/R&B duo featuring married couple Noopur (vocals, guitar, songwriting) and Alexander (piano, bass, production). Fusing two distinct cultures and musical backgrounds, India and Liechtenstein. Meeting in Zurich, marrying in Boston, and putting down roots in Berlin - the pair turn their relationship into the core foundation of their sound. Noopur's intimate storytelling melts seamlessly into Alexander's melody-driven production, creating a modern blend of Pop/R&B that is sticky, groovy, and thought-provoking.

https://linktr.ee/noopsssss

Smriti Sankar pop

Smriti Sankar is a Berlin-based singer, songwriter, and producer bringing bold, polished dance-pop to the stage. With sleek electronic production, powerful vocals, infectious melodies, and vulnerable lyrics, Smriti creates pop that is made to move, both on the dance floor and beneath the surface. Her sound combines confident, high-energy production with the intimacy of honest songwriting, creating music that feels both exciting and deeply personal. A Berklee College of Music graduate, Smriti writes, produces, and performs her own music, bringing a hands-on approach to every part of her artistry. Now based in Berlin, she is building a distinctly modern pop sound that invites listeners to feel, move, and embrace a little adventure, with music that feels fresh, vibrant, and unexpected.

https://linktr.ee/smritis
