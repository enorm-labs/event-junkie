The event takes place on the  8th of October starting at  20:00:00.

Rage’n’Bounce is back at Berlin’s Panke Culture as part of Tag der Clubkultur 2026 – Stay Core. Expect a genre-fluid night moving between rap, punk and bass, with live performances and DJ sets that blur the lines between concert, club and community space. At the centre of the night is the Safer Moshpit: a space to let loose, get loud and turn feminist rage into collective energy.Rage’n’Bounce puts queer BIPoC FLINTA* at the heart of the event, creating a space where they can take up space, be unapologetic and fully own the stage and dancefloor. Allies are warmly welcome too, as long as they respect and support the event’s focus. The night is designed with accessibility and safer-space practices in mind: the venue is wheelchair accessible, there are no strobe lights, and the indoor space is smoke-free. The bass-heavy programme also offers a physical and sensory way of experiencing the music for deaf and hard-of-hearing guests.
