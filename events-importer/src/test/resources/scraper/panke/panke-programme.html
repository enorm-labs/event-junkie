In the Neighbourhood” is an event taking place as part of the Pop-Kultur Festival, born out of the idea of working together with music industry professionals based in the immediate vicinity to organise an after-show event on 25 August to complement the Pop-Kultur Talks which are taking place beforehand.

Tue25Aug

The event takes place on the  25th of August starting at  21:00:00.

“In the Neighbourhood” is an event taking place as part of the Pop-Kultur Festival, born out of the idea of working together with music industry professionals based in the immediate vicinity to organise an after-show event on 25 August to complement the Pop-Kultur Talks which are taking place beforehand. We were able to secure the participation of the labels K7 and Sinnbus, as well as the booking agency Von der Hardt. Since its foundation in 1985 by the late Horst Weidenmüller, K7 has been an internationally successful company in the field of electronic music. They became particularly well known for their DJ-Kicks series. K7 is based on the Silent Green site. Sinnbus is a label that has been active since 2000; it evolved from a collective of various bands and is at home in both the indie and electro genres. The same building on Reinickendorfer Straße is home to the internationally active concert agency Von der Hardt, which primarily represents artists spanning the spectrum from neoclassical to electronic music. Each of the three companies is presenting one live act, and K7 is also providing a DJ.

Last Resort

M

The event takes place on the  25th of July starting at  23:00:00.

LAST RESORT returns with a summer edition exploring contemporary club music through rhythm, bringing together artists whose adventurous selections remain firmly rooted in the dancefloor. Miami’s Jonny from Space and DJ Fitness join forces for a B2B between two longtime collaborators whose psychedelic, percussion-led approach has become synonymous with one of North America’s most exciting underground scenes. Farsight, alys(alys)alys, Viiaan and Retter – four Berlin-based artists whose roots stretch from Peru, Brazil, Mexico and Colombia – are on the bill as well, each navigating club music through their own distinctive vocabulary of bass, breaks and groove. Together, the lineup reflects Berlin not as a single sound but as a meeting point where diverse musical histories converge on the dancefloor.

Amiruz is not a DJ, but a dedicated vinyl collector with a deep ear for Reggae and Dub. For the early hours, she’ll be serving a finely selected set of warm cuts and sundowner moods: strictly all killah, no fillah!

Skratch is a DJ devoted to the craft, with nearly 20 years spent sharpening his deck skills and digging deep through records. Moving between vinyl, dubplates, and digital, his sets combine precise mixing with a sharp ear for hidden gems and forward-thinking sounds. SECRET GUEST MC known for his machine gun style!

Rafush is a Brazilian-born, Berlin-based DJ and curator behind Ouch, connecting Afro-diasporic sounds, rare grooves, bass, and experimental club music through parties and radio shows. Their sets move with global rhythm, playful pressure, and a sharp underground instinct.
