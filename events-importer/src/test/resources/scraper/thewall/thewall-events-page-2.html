The Kee News — News-Based Comedy Show

Nov 7 4:00 PM

The Kee Comedy

Catch the wildest, funniest takes on today’s headlines live at …

Catch the wildest, funniest takes on today’s headlines live at The Kee News – your new fave news comedy night!

In the Soviet Union, November 7 was celebrated as Revolution Day. What do you think a joke with that setup should end with? Let’s gather in Berlin and find out together.

Join us on November 7 at The Wall for The Kee News - a smart, funny and slightly chaotic news-based comedy show where comedians turn headlines, politics, personal drama and global madness into stand-up.

This is not just a regular comedy showcase. The comedians will bring their own takes on politics, pop culture, Berlin life, expat chaos and everything weird happening in the world right now. Then the audience gets involved: write down the news that matters to you, and we’ll discuss it live on stage.

It can be anything - elections, scandals, celebrity gossip, a strange thing that happened at work, your flatmate joining a cult, or your mum suddenly becoming a political commentator. If it feels important to you, we can probably make it funny.

On stage:

Ludwig Benecke - a Swiss-German comedian who has performed at the Edinburgh Fringe Festival, opened for Victor Patrascan on tour and brings dry, clever comedy about culture, language and the weirdness of everyday life.

Tommy Ferrari - an Italian comedian performing across Europe with playful stories about life in Germany, Italian heritage and the everyday nonsense of trying to be a functioning adult.

Sandy Jones - a Bolivian-British comedian bringing high-energy, cheeky and sharp observations about Germany, culture shock and international life in English, Spanish and German.

The Kee - host of the show, comedian with 15 years of experience, creator of a YouTube late-night show, arrested for jokes in Russia and now making comedy in Berlin.
