The Wall Comedy

Grünberger Str. 84, Berlin, Berlin, 10245, DE

The Wall Comedy Club, Berlin was ironically based on a world with no more walls and is a tribute to the fall of the Berlin Wall in 1989. It is a place where anyone can come visit to share in our common sense of laughter and fun with some of the best comedians and artists from all over the world!

It is the perfect place for a night of laughs and great entertainment. We pride ourselves on being the go-to spot for stand-up comedy in Berlin, featuring some of the best of local and international acts. Our stage is alive with energy and laughter, giving audience members an unforgettable experience and a night of entertainment for both our open mic shows and showcase events. We hand-pick many of the best comedians from around the world, including the United States, Germany, the United Kingdom along with other places in Europe, North America, South America, parts of Asia and Australia.

On top of experiencing a great show, feel free to enjoy a beer, wine, soft drink, craft beer, or cocktail from one of our lovely bartenders at the bar, and we welcome you to stick around to meet our comedians after the show or even take pictures of yourself on our famous stage!

Whether you’re looking for a great night of out with friends or family, come to the Wall Comedy Club for a night of unforgettable comedy.

The Kee News — News-Based Comedy Show

Oct 3 5:30 PM

The Kee Comedy

Catch the wildest, funniest takes on today’s headlines live at …

Catch the wildest, funniest takes on today’s headlines live at The Kee News – your new fave news comedy night!

Looking for a smart, funny and slightly chaotic Saturday night in Berlin? Join us on October 3, where four international comedians turn headlines, personal drama and global madness into stand-up.

This is not just a regular comedy showcase. The comedians will bring their own takes on politics, pop culture, Berlin life, expat chaos and everything weird happening in the world right now. Then the audience gets involved: write down the news that matters to you, and we’ll discuss it live on stage.

It can be anything - politics, celebrity gossip, a strange thing that happened at work, your flatmate joining a cult, or your mum getting married for the second time. If it feels important to you, we can probably make it funny.

On stage:

Fitz - a New Zealand comedian performing across Europe with warm, punchy and absurd comedy about identity, everyday chaos and trying to stay normal in an increasingly weird world.

Ludwig Benecke - a Swiss-German comedian who has performed at the Edinburgh Fringe Festival, opened for Victor Patrascan on tour and brings dry, clever comedy about culture, language and the weirdness of everyday life.

Tom Strissel - an American comedian and actor, semi-finalist of the 2024 Berlin New Standup Award and member of the sketch comedy group Fuster Clucks.

Steph DePrez - an American comedian, emcee, singer and writer, bringing charming, sharp and personal comedy about performance, identity, social chaos and trying to connect with other humans.

The Kee - host of the show, comedian with 15 years of experience, creator of a YouTube late-night show, arrested for jokes in Russia and now making comedy in Berlin.

produced & usually hosted by ANNA BEROS (AUS)

Anna’s a Kiwi born, Melbourne raised, Berlin based full-time independent stand-up comedian, YouTube host, podcaster and producer. She celebrates life’s struggles with incisive stand-up and playful audience interaction. Her 3rd stand-up solo, High Hoe enjoyed a successful full run at the Edinburgh Fringe 2025 and her previous solos, Creampie Curious and Creampie Clarity played at numerous comedy festivals through the UK and Australia in 2023 and 2024. She has written for Comedy Central DE, headlines across Europe and produces and hosts 5 weekly shows in Berlin. In 2025, she opened for Jeff Arcuri at Admiralspalast, in 2024 for April Macie on US army bases in Germany and for Ian Fidance in Berlin in 2019. She hosts the comedy channel, Ersties Nonsense, and has the podcast Adults ONLY Comedy Berlin.

SASHA NEZLOBIN: “I’M FINE” - LIVE IN BERLIN

Oct 6 8:00 PM

The Wall Comedy Productions

Sasha Nezlobin was one of Russia’s biggest stand-up comedians — …

Sasha Nezlobin was one of Russia’s biggest stand-up comedians — until he left everything behind for one reason: freedom of speech. Now rebuilding his life and career in America, Sasha brings an unforgettable hour of comedy that’s sharp, fearless, personal, and surprisingly hilarious.

“I’m Fine” is a story about losing everything — your country, your career, your comfort — and discovering who you really are when you have to start over from nothing.

Dark, bold, honest, and deeply funny, Sasha blends cultural observations, personal stories, and hard-earned perspective into a comedy experience unlike anything else touring right now.
