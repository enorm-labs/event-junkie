Extrawelt – German Techno Pioneers & Live Innovators | Hypnotic Soundscapes with Driving Precision

Hamburg-based duo Extrawelt (Arne Schaffhausen & Wayan Raabe) have become icons of the global electronic scene, known for their deep, hypnotic sound that fuses techno, electro, and progressive elements. Their breakout on Border Community with “Soopertrack” marked the start of a career defined by boundary-pushing releases on labels like Cocoon, Kompakt, and Traum Schallplatten.

Renowned for their immersive live sets, Extrawelt balance club-ready intensity with cinematic storytelling, blending rolling basslines, intricate percussion, and atmospheric synths into seamless performances. Their sound has captivated audiences from Berlin’s underground clubs to the world’s biggest festival stages.

With their unmatched ability to merge experimentation with emotional depth, Extrawelt stand as one of the most influential and enduring duos in contemporary techno.

For fans of: Stephan Bodzin, Dominik Eulberg, Gui Boratto, progressive & melodic techno
