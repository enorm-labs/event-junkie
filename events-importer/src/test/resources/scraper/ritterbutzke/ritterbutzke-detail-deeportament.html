Deeportament Community is a cult event series from sunny Odessa, founded by DJ and producer Vlad Yaki. For more than 13 years, the project has promoted modern European electronic music culture in Ukraine, bringing together leading names in Indie Dance and Melodic Techno.

Now, Deeportament takes its next step onto the international stage with its first European edition — right in the heart of Berlin.

Belgian DJ and producer Nico Morano delivers melodic, uplifting house with a warm and emotional edge. Founder of Ontourage, he blends organic and progressive sounds into colourful sets that connect people on dance floors worldwide. With releases on Get Physical and Days Like Nights, more than 600K monthly Spotify listeners, and performances ranging from Tomorrowland to international stages across five continents, Nico Morano stands for energy, emotion, and togetherness.

Vlad Yaki – High-Energy House & Tech | Berlin via Ukraine A powerhouse of positive energy and infectious house grooves, Vlad Yaki rose to fame as one of Ukraine’s most in-demand DJs before bringing his signature sound to the heart of Berlin’s underground. Known for his ability to deeply connect with the crowd, Vlad delivers vibrant, high-energy sets that blend house, techno, and deep dancefloor rhythms. Now a resident at Klunkerkranich and a regular at iconic clubs like Sisyphos and Kater Blau, Vlad has quickly become a standout in Berlin’s club circuit. With appearances on Sunshine Live and gigs across Germany, his dynamic style is lighting up dancefloors from rooftops to warehouses. For fans of: Sisyphos vibes, Berlin house scene, feel-good techno, deep house
