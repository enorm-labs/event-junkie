Sven Ratzke

KOPFKINO

A journey through film and time

After his magnificent shows about legends such as Marlene Dietrich and David Bowie in sold-out theaters from Berlin to Amsterdam to New York, he now returns with his latest solo performance, KOPFKINO.

The charismatic singer, entertainer, and exceptional artist takes us on a magical journey through the world of cinema, spanning more than 130 years. In KOPFKINO, the stage becomes a living screen, drawing the audience into a universe where anything is possible…

Experience the dazzling dream world of film — from its wild beginnings to the golden age of Hollywood and the poetic visions of cinema masters such as Fellini, Fassbinder, Hitchcock, and Lynch. Well-known and lesser-known film songs are performed in unique new arrangements, while Ratzke brings divas and dreamers to life with his wildly imaginative conférencier style and charming anecdotes. At times humorous, at times deeply moving. Be surprised when, all of a sudden, Greta Garbo or Miss Piggy appear to come alive.

Ratzke’s deep passion for cinema is unmistakable in all his performances. His shows are an impressive blend of theater, cabaret, and captivating music, presented in a truly unique way. Let yourself be enchanted!
