14.10.2026 20:00 - Open End

 Orania.Piano

Plays the music of Tom Waits

An evening dedicated to one of the great individualists of American music. Blues, vaudeville, literature - Tom Waits could draw his inspiration from everything that came before his ears and eyes. Agita Rando approaches this music from her very own side, inspired by its highly individual beauty, in which the trivial and the abysmal are often inseparable.
