Thu 10/01/2026 08:00 pm Start 07:00 pm Doors Concert Kuppelhalle Ticket

silent green presents

Felisha Ledesma & Angelo Harmsworth
Felisha Ledesma and Angelo Harmsworth come together in a collaboration that unites a decade-long creative relationship. Informed by their years in American DIY circles, their new body of work bridges meditative intimacy and visceral abstraction. Ledesma’s warm, organic palette meets Harmsworth’s sharp textural contrasts and affective tendencies. Together, they create a space where deep listening and sheer physicality converge.


Francesco Corvi & Nocturnerror
Nocturnerror and Francesco Corvi collaborate together to explore data entanglement, both as a conceptual reference and as a sound design practice. Corvi’s in-depth research into live coding combines with Nocturnerror’s exploration of the spectres of the virtual age, giving rise to a cybernetic sound entity of sentient codes and ritual hymns.


Lighting Design by Chaeryung Yoon.
