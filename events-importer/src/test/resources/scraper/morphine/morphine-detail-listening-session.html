Gibrana Cervantes — verdad y error Listening Session
Presented by Radical Sounds Latin America
Friday, 02.10.2026, doors 19:00
Listening session + intervention: 19:30

verdad y error, out October 16 on AWOS.

For this evening at Morphine Raum, the album will be played in full, accompanied by a live intervention from Cervantes and Stephan Snider. The evening also features a DJ set by Leslie García, while DJ Putilla hosts and shapes the atmosphere around the listening session.

Conceived as an intimate gathering rather than a traditional concert, Cervantes invites the audience to spend time with the record collectively. Bringing some of the warmth of home into the space, she’ll welcome guests with mezcal from Guerrero, where she grew up, and a small aperitivo to accompany the listening.

verdad y error emerges from the tension between inner calm and the noise outside. Built around Cervantes’ expanded violin practice, the record moves through processed and multiplied strings, fragmented voices, distortion, static, and moments of stillness. Across the album, memories surface and disappear, searching for a space somewhere between clarity and confusion, truth and error.

The evening offers a first chance to hear the album together, ahead of its release.
