THE ASC (The Attention Span Crisis) is a Berlin-based quartet exploring the space between cinematic storytelling and raw emotional transmission. Founded by guitarist and composer Yair Karelic, the band draws on psychedelic rock, krautrock, post-rock and subtle Middle Eastern influences to create immersive, long-form compositions. Largely instrumental, yet punctuated by voice when words become unavoidable, the music unfolds through repetition, atmosphere and dynamic contrast, inviting listeners into a continuous journey rather than a collection of songs.
