ARCANE XIII: LA MORT

Trance, Techno

For this edition, Arcane XIII returns to Crack Bellmer for a new ritual through hypnotic techno, ambient, deep grooves, psychedelic textures and darker electronic sounds. Arcane XIII is a Berlin-based collective and podcast series, exploring underground electronic music through tarot, moon cycles, rituals and symbolic storytelling. This edition is dedicated to La Mort (aka Death or La Muerte) of the Tarot de Marseille, the card of endings, transformation and the beginning of a new cycle. Taking place under the Scorpio Sun, traditionally linked to Pluto, the night moves through themes of intensity, destruction, regeneration and rebirth. A portal between worlds, where something is left behind so something new begins to emerge.
