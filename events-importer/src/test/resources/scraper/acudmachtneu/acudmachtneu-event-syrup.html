SYRUP
FR 23.10, 22H ACUD CLUB
ENTRANCE: 15E

SYRUP is back in Berlin. Sweet. Sticky. Sinful.

On 23 October, we are taking over ACUD MACHT NEU for a night full of DJs, performances and unapologetic queer energy.

SYRUP is a sex positive FLINTA* & FRIENDS club night.
A space to dance, connect, explore and celebrate each other. Come exactly as you are. Feel save, feel free.

Our favorite spooky season is the best time to come come alone and stick together or come together and stick even more together. ;)

Expect deep bass, sweaty bodies, seductive performances and a dance floor where consent, respect and community come first.
