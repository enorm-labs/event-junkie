UNRUH & friends

24 OCTOBER 2026 / 21:00 – 03:00

ZK/U BerlinSiemensstr. 27, 10551, Berlin, Germany

Four years of UNRUH. Four years of films, ideas, friendships, long nights, experiments, mistakes, and a growing community of people who made all of it possible. This October, we’re bringing everyone together at ZK/U Berlin to celebrate where we’ve come from and everything that’s still ahead.
The night is an open invitation to the young Berlin film scene: to old friends and new faces, longtime collaborators and people meeting UNRUH for the first time.

Upstairs, the terrace becomes a space to hang out, meet, talk, philosophise. Downstairs, we celebrate with an all-female DJ lineup and two secret live acts. 

This is a celebration of the community around UNRUH and a chance to bring that community back into together. Come for a drink. Stay for a conversation. Meet someone you want to make a film with. Find an old friend. Make a new one and dance until 3.
