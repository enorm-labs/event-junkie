Residency

ZK/U – Center for Art and Urbanistics is an artist-led residency and production space for art and urban research in Berlin-Moabit, located near the Westhafen, Berlin’s largest inland port. Founded in 2012 in a former railway depot and recently renovated and expanded, ZK/U hosts interdisciplinary residencies of usually 2–6 months, offering living and working spaces for artists, researchers and practitioners exploring urban dynamics.
