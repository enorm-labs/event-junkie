HOSE presents: Gorz, Hassan K. & Restless

11 SEPTEMBER 2026 / 20:00 – 00:00

ZK/U BerlinSiemensstr. 27, 10551, Berlin, Germany

HOSE presents: 

Gorz 
gorzband.bandcamp.com 
gorz.online
instagram.com/gorz.band 

GORZ is a two part band born out of the urge to perform a loud and sometimes primitive setlist of riffs, distortion and contorting vocals while bringing drama, irony and intensity to the stage. With a hypnotic show, the duo builds elasticity in their sound, complexity in their format and fresh frenesi.

Hassan K.
hassank.bandcamp.com 
hassan-k.com

Hassan K. is a mystical one man band from Persia. Open source, DIY artist, Hassan mixes folklore and technologies, occident and orient, surf music and bellydance, swing and heavy metal… in crazy live acts, not too far from transe. With an augmented electric setar and a drum machine, he travels from the west kingdom to the Turan, spreading the words of his illuminated ancestors…

restless
wearerestless.bandcamp.com
instagram.com/we.are.restless

Restless is a Brazilian-Australian noisy punk duo playing loud, fast, and catchy music. Their lyrics blend personal experiences with political themes like pain, personal growth and the challenges of being separated from loved ones. The band consists of Adriessa Souza and Eilis Frawley, both deeply rooted in the Berlin independent scene. Their shared love for loud, groovy music brought them together.

This event is hosted by HOSE shows.

Entry by donation at the door. No pre-sale.
