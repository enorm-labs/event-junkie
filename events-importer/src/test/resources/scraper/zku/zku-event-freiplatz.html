FREIPLATZ presents: Doin’ It In The Park

FOOD & FOOTAGE – Independent movies in combination with food

21 OCTOBER 2026 / starting from 18:00

ZK/U BerlinSiemensstr. 27, 10551, Berlin, Germany

Freiplatz serves as the foundation and research focus for ALBA BERLIN’s upcoming 28.15 Basketball Museum. We are interested in basketball as a game, as a sociocultural phenomenon, and as a vibrant subculture.

As a research initiative, Freiplatz analyzes the physical, organizational, and historical dynamics of public basketball courts, primarily in Berlin but also worldwide. Through this work, we aim to map the complex relationships between urban planning, local grassroots communities, and other stakeholders.

In this context, the award winning documentary Doin’ It In The Park, exploring the rich history, culture, and global influence of pickup basketball in New York City will be screened at FOOD & FOOTAGE.

Doin’ It in the Park
by Bobbito Garcia and Kevin Couliau
83'00''
USA, 2013
English with English subtitles

Following the screening is a Q&A session with the director Kevin Couliau. The Paris-based photographer and filmmaker to uncover stories that highlight the profound impact of sports on individuals and society.

The film screening is free of charge; purchasing a meal is not required to attend the movie night.
The price is 15 euros per person (drinks not included).
