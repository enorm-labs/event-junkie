Just Signed! | with Yasola, Victoria West & PARA 

On Wednesday, November 18, 2026, we're bringing three exciting acts to Modus in Berlin who have recently signed their first record deals and are now ready to present their music live together for the very first time. Just Signed!, presented by Reeperbahn Festival and RBX GmbH, puts new voices, personal performances, and the shared discovery of emerging artists center stage. This is one you definitely don't want to miss!

Presented by Rausgegangen, Flux FM and DIFFUS. 

This time featuring:

  • Yasola (Warner Music): With her ethereal, versatile voice and a distinctive aesthetic of her own, Yasola creates a sound shaped by raw intimacy, deliberate contrasts and layers of emotion.
  • Victoria West (AWAL): Victoria West stands for an unconventional, contemporary pop sound that is every bit as colourful and maximalist as she is herself – always personal, confident and unafraid to be deliberately different.
  • PARA (Eintracht Pankow): Dresden-based artist PARA moves confidently between rap, indie and pop, combining raw directness with emotional clarity and a keen sense for atmosphere.

Doors open for the concerts at 7:30 p.m., and the first concert begins at 8:00 p.m.

More information: https://www.reeperbahnfestival.com/en/just-signed
