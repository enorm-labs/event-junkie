About the Concert

URBAN SPREE KLUBNACHT

25.09.26 21:00 — LATE

One night, two spaces. The night starts outside in the Urban Spree Garden at 21:00 with Albert Kraft, before moving inside to the Klub from 00:00 with Key Clef and Daraio.

GARDEN — 21:00 → 00:30 Albert Kraft —

DJ set KLUB — FROM 00:00 Key Clef — DJ set Daraio —

DJ set ALBERT KRAFT Berlin-based DJ and producer Albert Kraft’s sound is rooted in groove, rhythm and a deep love for underground electronic music. Originally from Mexico and raised in Ecuador, he has spent over a decade immersed in Berlin’s diverse club culture. With a background in sound engineering and as a studio guitarist, he approaches DJing with both technical precision and intuition. Rooted in techno, his sets move fluidly through house, deep, soulful and experimental sounds, with a constant focus on groove and storytelling. Whether creating a warm, laid-back atmosphere or taking the dancefloor into deeper territory, his selections are driven by feeling, musicality and a timeless approach to the underground.

KEY CLEF Key Clef is a Berlin-based Italian DJ and producer with a strong passion for analogue instruments and the founder of the label Ipnotica Erotica. After experiencing the final years of Rome’s flourishing rave scene and earning a degree in sound engineering, her creative practice evolved across club music, performance art and electroacoustics. Her productions and live performances combine synths, drum machines and analogue effects, while her DJ sets reveal the full spectrum of her musical taste and deep knowledge of the dancefloor. Moving freely between styles and hybrid forms, Key Clef creates an ongoing dialogue between sound, emotion and audience.

DARAIO Daraio’s artistic practice spans vinyl-only DJ sets, analogue live performances and experimental shows built around self-designed musical instruments. Rather than being confined to a single genre, his sound moves through ambient, techno, minimal, house with dub influences, bass music and drum & bass, creating immersive and unpredictable sonic journeys. Founder of Tensione Collective and the SWAV production, mixing and mastering studio in Bari, his multidisciplinary practice brings together music, technology and artistic research. This approach has taken him to clubs and festivals across Europe as well as scientific and educational contexts such as TED.

    About

    • Galerie
      • Galerie
      Within Urban Spree, the Urban Spree Galerie is a 400 sqm independent contemporary urban art space. Set in a 70.000 sqm postindustrial compound in the heart of Berlin, the gallery benefits from its large urban grassroots ecosystem and offers its invited artists and photographers an ideal space for experimentation through ambitious on-site residencies. The gallery curates independent art shows on a monthly basis, which most likely include the painting of the whole compound.

    • Bookshop
      • Bookshop

      The Urban Spree Bookshop offers a selection of graffiti, street art and photography books 


      The Urban Spree Bookshop is located in the gallery and displays a hand-picked, organic selection of street art, graffiti, urban photography books, and self-published zines. Supporting the local artist ecosystem and analog culture, Urban Spree displays local artistic products and also produces screen prints, t-shirts, and other limited edition crafted objects. In addition, Urban Spree Books publishes books of photography and art on a regular basis.

    • The Wall
      • The Wall
      Urban Spree's flagship “Artist Wall“ is a 15 m long x 8 m high wall surface facing Warschauer Str., one of the busiest arteries of Berlin, counting 100.000 visitors/day. Artists who painted the wall in the past include Above, Klone, Zevs, Broken Fingaz Crew, Twoone, Low Bros, Nychos, 1UP Crew, Rylsee, m-city, Johannes Mundinger, Jim Avignon, Mik Shida, Anthony Lister, Daze, Lars Wunderlich and many more.

    • Artists Residencies
      • Artists Residencies
      Artist Residencies are at the core of our artistic ecosystem. A mix of permanent, medium term and guest studios, the residencies irrigate by their creativity and artistic contributions what Urban Spree is about. Currently, our residents include Rylsee, Johannes Mundinger, howtokillagraffiti, Lars Wunderlich and Dafne Tree. Special Resident in Summer 2026 is David Morago.
