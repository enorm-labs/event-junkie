What is 800A Berlin?

Berlin's multicultural creative community finds a home at 800A in Wedding, an international performance space that celebrates the diversity of Berlin and the artistic variety that it offers.

We regularly host world-class performances and empower talented newcomers with stage time. We love theatre, burlesque, comedy, dance, drag, film, magic, music, poetry, art.

We're always open to hosting new and varied performances and shows, and we help people unleash their inner creativity through our classes, whether Electronic Music, Acting or German Language.

Since 2017, our space has hosted hundreds of amazing shows and artists like Queer Science Theatre, Extravagant Shambles Burlesque, ComedyShorts Improv, Queer Bedroom Pop, Sam Jay, Judah Friedlander, Lizzy and The Palm, Bye Beneco, Instant Theatre Berlin, Kaleidoscope Musical, Drunk Classics, Peppe Voltarelli, The Richard P. Dick Van Johnson Show, and many more.

We were awarded the yearly award and were selected to be official hosts of the Tag der Clubkultur 2024 by the Berliner Clubcommission.

We come from all over the world, and we work every day for our collective future by providing free meeting space and affordable conditions for local artists who've chosen Berlin as their cultural home.
