Ah! My Kid’s A German – Stand Up Comedy in English

Foto/Flyer © promo – Veranstalter: Adam Shor, Berlin

“I knew raising my kid in Germany would be amazing, I just didn’t expect him to become a German”

Ah, My Kid’s a German! is a sharp, personal stand-up show about expat life, marriage, fatherhood, cultural identity, and the strange experience of building a family somewhere that still occasionally feels foreign. American comedian Adam Shor — one half of the comedy duo Adam and Eyal — thought moving to Germany would be the culture shock. Then he got married, became a father, and realized identity gets a lot messier when cultures collide at home. Expect an intimate night of smart, slightly inappropriate storytelling—the kind of comedy that feels like having a drink with a funny friend who has made a series of questionable international life choices.
