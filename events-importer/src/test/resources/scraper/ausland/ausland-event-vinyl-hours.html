Vinyl Hou(r)s Record Club #05 with Andrea Neumann

Andrea Neumann

/// Vinyl Hou(r)s Record Club #05 with Andrea Neumann ///

This gathering is about listening, together. 
Each time, one Navigator is invited.
They bring the carefully chosen selection of records from their collection.

The only request we make is simple:
present one side of a record, and listen to the entire side together.

Find your corner. Settle in with a drink — sometimes there are chips and vegan brownies.
Feel free to bring something to share.

Put your headphones away for a while.
Getting back the warmth of vinyl.
Music shared, time slowed, presence honored.

 

We start at 15:00 and end 19:00

▪️ This is not a live concert ▪️

Donation suggestion: € 5,00 - € 20

Location: ausland
Lychener Straße 60, 10437 Berlin, Deutschland!
