Éclat Crew - music making meetup for FLINTA

Are you a FLINTA+ producer looking for like-minded music-makers? Having a hard time finding time to make music? Still learning your DAW? Or are you not even ready to call yourself a producer yet? 

 

Get out of your bedroom and come and meet makers, learn new skills, make music, and connect with a community in a friendly, relaxed space.

 

Éclat Crew is a Berlin-based Electronic Music Collective aiming to support, promote, and inspire the practice and output of FLINTA* (female, lesbian, intersex, non-binary, trans, and agender) artists of all levels of experience through collaboration and cooperation.

We meet fortnightly in Berlin to work on shared music-making challenges. We also throw parties, and collaborate with other collectives and events around Berlin and Europe.

Ready to join? Learn how to participate and sign up to our next workshops or keep reading:


https://forms.gle/PVVG3AsMRBD7uADu5 

 

Are you a FLINTA+ person person interested in electronic music production?
Come to our IRL Workshop at ausland!

 

It's a chance to connect, chat, share your music, make some music, and hear some of your peers share some of their knowledge. Our gear library will be there for all your gear-borrowing desires, and it's a lovely chance to reconnect with your fellow music-makers for the first time in 2026.

 

Free entry, registration required.

18:00 - Event start, intros & explanations
18:30 - music making!
20:30 - Celebratory listening session
22:00 - End

 

Covid 19: Éclat events are Covid-cautious spaces. This means that we ask all participants to take a covid test on the day of the workshop before arrival and bring a photo of the negative test. Tests can also be provided for 1-2€ upon arrival. If you need to take a test at the space, please arrive at least 15 minutes prior to the start of the event. Masking is welcome but not mandatory.

Accessibility: there will be a person helping with accessibility topics (if possible write us at buero@ausland.berlin in advance with a clear description of what you need, showing up without writing also ok, but then we have to wing it a bit). The ventilation will be on during the whole event.
