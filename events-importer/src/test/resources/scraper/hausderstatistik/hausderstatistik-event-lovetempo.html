Freitag 23.10.26

lovetempo (NYC / Mattie Safer of The Rapture) + Moriah Plaza

lovetempo is the soul-drenched, „lonely-hearts disco“ project of Brooklyn-based artist Mattie Safer (formerly of The Rapture and longtime touring member of Poolside). The project blends saudade sentimentality and shimmering disco with sophisticated house, jazz-funk, and global rhythms to create a sound that is heartfelt, euphoric, and made to move you.

With a catalog featuring releases on Razor-N-Tape, Too Slow To Disco, Delusions of Grandeur, and Toucan Sounds, lovetempo has garnered consistent support from tastemakers including Gilles Peterson (BBC 6Music), Laurent Garnier, Melvo Baptiste (Glitterbox), and Colleen ‚Cosmo‘ Murphy (Balearic Breakfast). The debut full-length album, There Is A Light, was released April 17, 2026, in partnership with Bastard Jazz Recordings.

Support:
Moriah Plaza (Berlin)

Brazilian soul, psych, bossa, and jazz, reimagined from Berlin, via the Dead Sea, on Moriah Plaza’s dreamy first album for Batov Records.
